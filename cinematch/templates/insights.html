How Recommendation Scores Work

CineMatch shows a simple percentage for every recommended Bollywood movie. Here is what that percentage means—and how it is calculated in plain English.

Important: Relative Match Score vs. Probability

The percentage next to each movie is a Relative Match Score (0–100%), not a guaranteed probability that you will enjoy the movie. Because our database has only a small number of ratings per Bollywood film (1 to 10 likes), a raw 1-out-of-1 match would falsely look like "100% certainty." Instead, CineMatch smooths small counts and combines viewer overlap, genre similarity, and dataset popularity into a balanced comparison score.

1 Viewer Overlap (Shared Likes)

First, CineMatch checks our database to see if people who rated your selected movie 4.0 stars or higher also positively rated another Bollywood movie.

Smoothed Viewer Score = Shared Viewers / (Total Viewers of Movie A + 2)

Adding + 2 (Laplace smoothing) keeps single-user overlaps modest (e.g., 1 of 1 = 33.3% rather than an exaggerated 100%).

2 Genre Match & Popularity Balance

Genre Similarity

Every Bollywood movie has genres like Comedy, Drama, Romance, or Musical. CineMatch measures how many genres a candidate movie shares with your selected movies (Jaccard similarity).

When Viewer Overlap Is Zero

If a movie (such as Munna bhai M.B.B.S.) has 0 recorded user ratings in the dataset, CineMatch falls back cleanly to Genre Similarity + Bollywood Popularity so you still get helpful Bollywood matches without any unrelated Hollywood movies.

3 Combining Everything Into One Score

When shared viewer ratings exist between your picks and a candidate movie, CineMatch starts from a viewer-overlap tier and combines:

Match Score = 32% Base Overlap + 38% Smoothed Viewer Score + 20% Genre Match + 5% Popularity + 5% Hindi Focus

When no shared viewer ratings exist yet, the score relies on Genre Match (65%), Popularity (20%), and Hindi Focus (15%) scaled to at most 45%—ensuring movies backed by real viewer overlap always rank higher than genre-only matches.
